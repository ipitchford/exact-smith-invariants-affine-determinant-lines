programs were developed within one AI-assisted producer process.

- exact mathematical proofs with internal adversarial review;
- 129,352 positive exact symbolic comparisons;
- 18 detected deliberate negative controls;
- normal and `python -O` execution;

- verified bibliography and claim contexts; and
- deterministic archive and safe fresh-extraction interfaces.

establish independent reproduction, completed Lean formalisation, absolute
novelty or priority, external peer review, journal acceptance, or mathematical
truth. GitHub, Zenodo, Evidence Press, graphics, audio, hashes, CI, and public
readback do not increase the mathematical assurance level.
