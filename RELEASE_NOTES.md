# Version 0.1.0-candidate — 9 August 2026

Initial public unrefereed candidate release.

## Headline result

The complete pairwise-resultant character lattice has an exact local
one-generator presentation. This determines every prime-adic valuation of its
final Smith invariant, gives a symmetric global formula and `O(k^2)` gcd
algorithm, proves that spanning-tree minors generate the full maximal-minor
gcd, and yields the nonprimitive Smith form
`diag(g, ..., g, g h(e))`.

## Supporting theorem package

- integral affine all-factor Plücker identity;
- multi-border determinant contraction;
- weighted tree and odd-unicyclic character-minor formula;
- residual diagonalizable group-scheme and exact bad-prime interpretation;
- finite locally free generic degree synthesis for square normalisations.

## Boundaries

The square/monic multiplication Jacobian, scalar resultant product,
multifactor rank criterion, signed-incidence support, arithmetic-matroid gcd
rule, and root-partition count are classical inputs. No arbitrary normaliser,
affine-slice, Keller-map, Hessian, or Jacobian-conjecture theorem is claimed.
