## Mathematical judgement

The revision resolves the first round's controlling objection rather than
merely renaming it. For primitive (mathbf e), the complete cross-weighted
edge-character cokernel has the local presentation

\[
 C_{\mathbf e}\otimes\mathbb Z_{(p)}
 \cong
 \mathbb Z_{(p)}/
 (D_a,\,2e_ie_j:i<j,\ i,j\ne a)
\]

for any (p)-unit pivot (a). This computes every (p)-adic valuation of
the saturation index. The paper then assembles a symmetric global formula,
proves that spanning-tree minors already determine the full maximal-minor gcd,
derives the complete nonprimitive Smith form, and identifies the connected and
prime-to-(p) étale factors of the residual diagonalizable group.

This constitutes the programme's Level-3 Smith-normal-form principle for the
complete pairwise-resultant architecture. It is more than the original
rank-one degree-difference principle and more than an unevaluated
determinantal divisor. It is not yet a general classification of arbitrary
character lists, arbitrary polynomial semi-invariants, or factorisation-derived
Keller maps.

The affine Pluecker theorem remains a useful integral, coordinate-complete
lift of classical square multiplication-Jacobian identities. The generic
degree theorem is correctly presented as a finite-locally-free synthesis of
the classical labelled root-partition cover with a torus isogeny. Neither is
used to claim an affine-space slice, Keller counterexample, Hessian result, or
Jacobian-conjecture consequence.
