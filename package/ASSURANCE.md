## Fresh-extraction interpretation

The producer fresh-extraction gate first matches the exact inspected archive
bytes to an externally supplied SHA-256, then verifies the manifest digest
before executing payload code. It checks all payload hashes, claim/evidence
references, canonical receipt parity, full exact replay, and manuscript build.
The PDF embeds a build timestamp, so a successful rebuild is not represented as
byte-for-byte PDF reproducibility. The extraction receipt is external to the
archive and does not certify independence or a newly reconstructed environment.

## Interpretation rules

1. A passing script is evidence about the encoded computation, not a proof of
   novelty or an independent theorem verification.
2. Two implementations written within one producer workflow can share a
   specification error and are not an independent reproduction.
3. A future Lean build would certify the encoded theorem under its declared
   axioms; it would not by itself certify prose correspondence, novelty, or
   peer review.
4. The exact classical square/monic multiplication-Jacobian, rank-locus,
   signed-graphic, and arithmetic-matroid antecedents must be attributed. A
   bounded search supports only “not found in the bounded search” for the
   narrower affine Plücker and exact cross-weighted local/global quotient
   statements, never “first,” “novel,” or “unique” without a separate
   qualified basis.
5. Generic scheme degree, reduced geometric point count, and etaleness are
   distinct in bad characteristic.
6. The local determinant theory does not recognise an affine-space slice or
   produce a Keller or Hessian theorem.
