## Imported mathematical datum

For binary forms $A\in V_r$ and $B\in V_s$, the parent states the complete
signed maximal-minor vector identity for the coefficient multiplication
differential. In its block-major, ascending-coefficient convention, the common
scalar is the Sylvester resultant and the kernel vector is $(A,-B)$, with
orientation factor $(-1)^{r(s+1)}$.

The child programme uses this theorem as the rank-one base case and proposes a
separate all-factor proof and formalisation. The child must restate every
coefficient, Sylvester, source-order, and deletion-sign convention rather than
refer to the parent informally.
