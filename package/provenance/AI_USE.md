# AI-use disclosure

AI systems were used during this research workflow for source discovery,
mathematical exploration, proof development, exact-check implementation,
adversarial testing, drafting, and construction of this research package.

The mathematical arguments and programs were produced and checked within one
AI-assisted producer process. Agreement between models or between separately
written programs in that process is not described as independent
reproduction. Exact checks are retained as structural evidence, with negative
controls and optimized-mode execution, but they do not replace mathematical
review.

Bibliographic claims require source-level checking, and novelty language is
limited to the bounded-search record. The responsible human contributor
authorised public release on 9 August 2026 under the same anonymous authorship
convention as the parent candidate. AI systems are not authors.

The package should preserve a traceable distinction between:

* AI-assisted generation;
* producer-side verification;
* Lean kernel checking;
* independent reproduction;
* specialist priority review; and
* peer review and publication.

No later automation should remove or weaken this disclosure without a recorded
human decision.
