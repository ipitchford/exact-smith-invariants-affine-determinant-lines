# Literature Strategy and Evidence Matrix

## 1. Stage-1 handoff and re-audit status

The initial Stage-1 search established the scalar polynomial-CRT,
generalized-Sylvester, determinant-of-complexes, projective-factorisation and
incidence-matrix background. It did not, however, resolve the more direct
coefficient-product Jacobian and polynomial-singularity literature. Treating
the original corpus as complete would therefore have created a pipeline-level
Mode-7 frame-lock: later drafting would have preserved an over-broad novelty
frame inherited from an incomplete early source map.

On 9 August 2026 a focused object-identity re-audit was run before allowing the
priority boundary to stand. It added five exact-object sources and corrected
the Stacks Project locators. The re-audit found no fatal exact collision with
the non-monic all-factor Plücker tensor, multi-border formula, or weighted
complete-edge Smith calculation. It did find direct classical antecedents for
square and normalised two-factor multiplication Jacobians, and strong
antecedents for arbitrary-factor monic rank loss. The defensible contribution
boundary is revised accordingly.

The parent Evidence Press candidate and this follow-up share a producer
process. Neither the earlier search nor this re-audit is an independent
specialist priority review.

## 2. Search families covered

1. coefficient-product, Cauchy-product and convolution-map Jacobians;
2. monic, one-factor-normalised and fully affine polynomial multiplication;
3. product equations, Hensel lifting and Sylvester/resultant Jacobians;
4. polynomial Chinese-remainder determinants and pairwise resultants;
5. generalized Sylvester, Koszul, Fitting and determinant-of-complexes
   resultant constructions;
6. coincident-root loci, polynomial-multiplication singularities and
   projective tangent criteria;
7. bordered, augmented and multi-border determinant identities;
8. projective polynomial multiplication, labelled root partitions and
   factorisation quotients;
9. torus characters, semi-invariants, finite diagonalizable kernels and
   generic factorisation degree;
10. unoriented and weighted incidence minors, odd-unicyclic determinants and
    Smith forms; and
11. exact statement and alias searches for the non-monic all-factor Plücker
    tensor, its contraction, and the complete resultant-character lattice.

Inclusion required an identifiable primary publication, publisher record,
stable authoritative technical reference, or the immutable parent candidate.
Secondary summaries were used only to locate primary texts. “Not found” means
not located in this bounded producer search, not proof of priority.

## 5. Remaining literature risk

No fatal collision was located. The principal unresolved risk is an implicit
equivalent of the all-factor Plücker tensor or weighted complete-edge Smith
calculation in determinant-of-complexes, Hensel-lifting,
polynomial-singularity, toric-quotient, logarithmic-derivation, free-divisor,
prehomogeneous-vector-space, quiver-representation, or arithmetic-graph
language. A specialist citation-graph and object-identity audit remains
necessary before any absolute novelty or priority claim.
