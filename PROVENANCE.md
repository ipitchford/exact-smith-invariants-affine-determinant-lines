## Child

This repository is a separately versioned extension. The parent supplies the
rank-one bordered-Jacobian base case and motivation. The child gives a
self-contained integral all-factor proof and develops the exact complete-edge
character-lattice Smith theory. It neither silently revises the parent nor
claims independent reproduction of it.

Detailed claim lineage, literature matrices, review records, and AI-use
disclosure are included under `package/provenance/` and `package/review/`.
